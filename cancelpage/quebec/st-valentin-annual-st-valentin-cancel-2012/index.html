Notes
According to the postmaster, BP St-Valentin has received a new design from Canada Post every year since it started offering pictorial cancels around 1994–1995; the previous year's rubber stamps go back to the warehouse to be destroyed. The office receives thousands of mail-in postmarking requests from all over the world for Valentine's Day, often well into the summer. Available in black and red ink.

See it on the map

This cancel is retired, so the post office can no longer apply it. Covers with it turn up from dealers and other collectors.
